THE ALCHEMICAL ALAKH

Hello! I’m Alakh Satsangi. 🌱 I am a student who loves learning, exploring new ideas, and creating things. I have a strong interest in science, nature, art, technology, and education. I completed my Class 10 from Jayanarayan Saraswati Vidya Mandir Inter College, Bareilly, and my Class 12 from Radhasoami Educational Institute Intermediate College, Dayalbagh, Agra. I created THE ALCHEMICAL ALAKH as my personal digital space to share my learning, study resources, creative work, stories, and ideas. My goal is not just to learn by myself, but to build a community where we can learn together, support each other, and grow together. 🌱🤝 I am also learning web development and building this website step by step. 🚀 Learn • Explore • Create • Grow Together ✨
